Dec 5 , 04:00 EST Resolved - Between 3:48am (US Eastern) and 4:10am (US Eastern) Cloudflare experienced an outage which affected Klaviyo. During this time, users were unable to access Klaviyo.com and all Klaviyo APIs. During this window, a number of services were affected.
